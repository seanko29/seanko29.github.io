Hi! I'm Sean 👋 — I am currently working as an AI Research Engineer at AiM Future. Previously, I was an Applied Artificial Intelligence graduate student @ Sungkyunkwan University, researching in MAIN Lab, advised by Prof. Jaekwang Kim. I also was an SKT AI Fellowship member (4th generation) with the SKT Supernova team in 2022. I received my B.S. degree in Molecular Biotechnology @ Ghent University Global Campus and wrote my bachelor thesis under the supervision of Dr. Utku Ozbulak and Prof. Wesley De Neve. My research interests center on efficient & generative deep learning — diffusion & flow-matching models, quantization & knowledge distillation, and VLMs, VLAs, and LLMs.

Feel free to reach out if you'd like to chat or collaborate!
